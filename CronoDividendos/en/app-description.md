# About CronoDividendos

**CronoDividendos** is a smart pension portfolio simulator designed for investors who want to plan wealth accumulation and receive long-term passive income, inspired by the best philosophies of evergreen investments.

## Main Features

*   **Organization by Cycles:** Define and simulate your financial goals macroscopically, watching time work in favor of your equity.
*   **Local Target Management:** Register your favorite assets and set clear quantity or contribution goals to track your progress visually.
*   **Earnings History:** Record dividends and interest on equity (JCP) received to measure the actual growth of your snowball effect.
*   **Shielded Privacy:** Your financial data belongs only to you. The entire database is stored 100% locally on your smartphone, operating anonymously and without the need for registration.

## Plans and Monetization

*   **Free Plan:** Access main simulation and control tools with support for footer banners and full-screen ads, limited to registering up to 2 simultaneous assets.
*   **Premium Version (Optional Upgrade):** Unlock unlimited stock or real estate fund registration and completely remove all ads from the app through a single, lifetime payment via Google Play Billing.
