# Privacy Policy - CronoDividendos

This Privacy Policy describes how the **CronoDividendos** application, developed by **FIPI Solutions**, handles your information when using our free service with an optional in-app purchase (Premium).

## 1. Local Data Storage (Total Privacy)
CronoDividendos is designed under the principle of total user data sovereignty. All information you enter into the application — including stock codes (tickers), real estate funds, target contributions, buy/sell transaction logs, and dividend history — is stored **strictly locally** on your device's internal database (using SQLite/Room technology).
* We do not send your portfolio data to external servers.
* We do not collect, sell, or share your simulated financial information.

## 2. Personal Information Collection
The application does not require any type of registration, account creation, login by email or social networks, nor does it request sensitive data such as social security numbers or bank passwords. You use the tool completely anonymously.

## 3. Third-Party Services and Ads
To keep the application free in its base version, we use third-party services that may collect anonymous identifiers from your device for advertising purposes:
* **Google AdMob:** Provides contextual ads in the form of footer banners and full screens (Intersticials). AdMob uses the standard Android advertising ID to ensure ads are safe and relevant, operating in compliance with Google guidelines.

## 4. Billing Security (Premium Version)
The application offers an optional lifetime Premium license purchase. The entire payment process, bank mediation, and purchase validation are handled exclusively by the official **Google Play Store (Google Play Billing)** platform. FIPI Solutions does not have access to your credit card data or billing information.

## 5. System Permissions
The application requests only **Internet Access** permission, strictly necessary to load Google AdMob advertising pieces and consult the status of your Premium license with the Google Play Store.

## 6. Changes and Contact
This policy may be updated periodically to reflect app improvements. Any changes will be published directly on this page.

If you have questions about our privacy practices, please contact us by email: **apps@fipi.solutions**
